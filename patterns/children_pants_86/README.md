# Size 86: roomy children's outer trousers

An original starting block inspired by the reference's roomy pull-on silhouette, with separate folded ribbing replacing the elastic casings. It is a **first fitting draft**, not a professionally fitted or graded commercial pattern. Use a sample in fabric with similar behaviour and fit it over the intended leggings before cutting the final outer fabric.

## Measurements and design choices

All dimensions are centimetres, measured on stitching lines unless stated otherwise.

| Item | Value |
|---|---:|
| Nominal height size | 86 |
| Body waist / hip | 50 / 55 |
| Body waist to floor, at side | 41 |
| Body vertical crotch to floor, over diaper and leggings | 25 |
| Finished hip | 65 (10 total ease) |
| Shell waist opening | 65.24, gathered into ribbing |
| Developed side length including ribbing | 44 (41 body reference + 3 ease) |
| Finished waistband / cuff heights | **3 / 3** |
| Shell side seam | 38 |
| Front crotch level below finished side waist | 19 (16 dressed body rise + 2 baseline drop + 1 additional ease) |
| Back centre waist rise above side | 2 |
| Matched front/back inseam seam lengths | 22.94 |
| Approximate upper thigh at crotch tips | 40.75 per leg |
| Shell ankle opening before gathering | 24 per leg |
| Assumed body ankle | **16, provisional** |
| Relaxed finished rib waistband loop | **47, provisional** |
| Relaxed finished cuff loop | **18, provisional** |
| Seam allowance | 1 throughout, generated by Seamly2D |

Both vertical body measurements refer to the child wearing a diaper and leggings. Their difference gives a 16 cm dressed body rise. The 25 cm measurement is a **vertical height**, not a seam to copy onto the inseam. No second diaper thickness correction is added. The draft retains 2 cm baseline crotch drop, adds 1 cm more rise ease, and adds 2 cm leg length relative to the floor-based draft. Both rise additions are movement ease beyond the already dressed measurements. Actual blousing above ankle cuffs also includes the unmeasured ankle-to-floor distance; it cannot be predicted exactly from the supplied measurements. The front shell has 22 cm vertical height below its crotch tip; the diagonal inseam is therefore 22.94 cm. The back tip is lowered about 1.48 cm to match inseam lengths; the back rise is also extended for seat coverage. The outer seams are straight and equal. These are deliberate simple block choices; refine the crotch and leg shape after fitting.

The front and back quarter hip widths are 15.75 and 16.75. There are no darts, fly, pockets, or reflective bands. The reference's reflective trim can be applied later without changing the block. Size 86 is a label for this baseline, **not an automatic grading rule**. The child will wear a diaper and leggings. Check sitting rise, seat room and squatting in that same combination during fitting; the hip/waist inputs and generous ease remain provisional until this movement check.

2. Open the **Variables** table (menu wording may vary by release). Inputs are `#waist`, `#hip`, `#outseam`, `#inseam`, `#hip_ease`, `#crotch_drop`, `#rise_ease`, `#leg_ease`, `#band_height`, `#cuff_height`, `#back_raise`, `#hem`, `#ankle`, `#cuff_ease`, `#waist_ratio`, and `#sa`. Descriptions distinguish measurements, estimates, and derived values. Do not edit derived values unless changing the construction method.
3. Select each draft block to adjust points or the crotch spline handles. Front/back/rib pieces are already defined in Pieces mode, including seam allowances. Grain arrows are parallel to the outer seam. Check grain alignment after changes.
4. Save your version under a new filename. Walk side and inseams again after changing curves. For large measurement changes, review the entire draft; this is not validated across a size range.
5. Generate your own print layout at **100% scale**; verify a known 10 cm length before cutting. The SVG is a visual reference, not a tiled print layout.

The native file is self-contained: dimensions live in its Variables table rather than a linked `.smis` file. This makes it convenient to share and adjust in Seamly2D.

## Cut list

Seamly2D adds **1 cm seam allowance on every edge**; the native piece outline distinguishes stitching and cutting lines. Do not add another allowance.

| Piece | Cut | Fabric / cut dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| Front | 2 mirrored | Main outer fabric; grain parallel to side seam |
| Back | 2 mirrored | Main outer fabric; grain parallel to side seam |
| Waistband | 1 rectangle | Ribbing **49 × 8 cm**, including allowances |
| Ankle cuff | 2 rectangles | Ribbing **20 × 8 cm** each, including allowances |

Ribbing rectangles are full lengths, not pieces cut on the fold. Greatest stretch runs along the **49 cm / 20 cm direction**; rib wales run across the 8 cm height. Fold lengthwise halfway across the 8 cm height (4 cm from the raw edge): after joining with 1 cm allowances, the visible band is 3 cm tall. The fold is shown as a construction line in Draft mode; mark it on the cloth. There are no deep hem turn-ups or elastic channels.

### Check the actual firm ribbing before committing to these lengths

- The waistband loop is 47 cm relaxed. It needs about **17% extension** just to pass the measured 55 cm body hip, plus comfortable clearance; attaching it evenly to the shell needs about **39% extension**. Check that it still recovers and supports the trousers comfortably.
- Each cuff loop is 18 cm relaxed, using a 16 cm estimated ankle plus 2 cm ease. Attaching to the shell needs about **33% extension**. Check comfortable passage over the actual heel/foot; ankle circumference alone cannot establish this.
- If the ribbing cannot stretch enough to attach, **gather the shell first**, then sew it to the ribbing. If the finished rib loop cannot pass the body part comfortably, increase the loop length. Changing the gathering does not solve an undersized opening.
- Try pinned/basted loops on the child before sewing. Firm ribbing may require a longer waistband than the provisional 47 cm. Adjust `#waist_ratio` for waistband length and `#ankle` / `#cuff_ease` for cuff length.

## Suggested assembly and fitting

1. Mark grain, hip reference level, front/back identity and rib folds. Cut mirrored pairs. Mark matching quarter points on shell waist and ribbing and matching halves/quarters on cuffs; match side seams explicitly because front/back widths differ slightly.
2. Sew each front to its corresponding back at the side seam, then the inseam, using 1 cm allowance. Both side seams are 38 cm; both inseams are 22.94 cm. Finish seam allowances to suit the fabric.
3. Put the legs right sides together and sew the continuous front-to-back crotch seam. Check the scoop for smooth sewing at the inseam junction.
4. Baste the waistband and cuffs for a fitting over leggings. Check sitting, squatting, hip passage, ankle/heel passage, seat coverage, and finished length. The cuffs should sit at the ankle. The 44 cm developed length is longer than the measured waist-to-floor reference deliberately: it is not a proposed cuff position 3 cm below the floor. Let the shell blouse above the cuffs and check the ankle position on the child. The waistband-to-ankle distance was not measured, so adjust `#leg_ease` at fitting if the fabric pools excessively or pulls when squatting. Floor measurements alone cannot fix this exactly.
5. Sew each rib rectangle's short ends into a loop, fold wrong sides together, then attach both raw long edges to the shell, distributing fullness. Use a stretch-compatible stitch and avoid stretching the shell. Gather the shell first if necessary. Finish allowances toward the shell.
